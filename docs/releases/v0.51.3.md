# TradeHelp 0.51.3

Review your latest trading day and view trade screenshots in AI Coach.

## AI Coach

- Ask to review your latest session to select the most recent logged trading day within your account, symbol and date filters.
- View screenshots attached to trades cited in a reply. Browse multiple images, click to enlarge, or open the trade.
- Screenshot previews stay on your device. The coach receives attachment counts, not image contents, and does not analyse the images in conversation.
- The coach receives tag counts from all matching trades, even when it can only read a sample. Revenge tags on the same trade count once.
- Read coach replies and citation links with consistent typography and shorter formatting.

Existing saved replies keep their original text. Ask a new question to use the updated session selection.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** `TradeHelp-Setup.exe`
- **macOS, Intel and Apple Silicon:** `TradeHelp-Setup.dmg`. TradeHelp uses the ZIP for automatic updates.
- **Linux:** `TradeHelp-Setup.AppImage`

GitHub publishes the release after all three platform builds and all 10 required installer, blockmap and update-manifest assets pass verification.

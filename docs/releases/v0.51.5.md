# TradeHelp 0.51.5

Search more of your written journal in AI Coach.

## AI Coach

- Search full trade-note text, saved reviews, daily notes and playbook text locally. The coach receives selected excerpts, with truncation disclosed.
- Request a whole-journal review without inheriting the previous session. Explicit account, symbol and date filters still apply.
- Review monthly totals calculated by the app and trade examples from across the selected timeline.
- See how many written records the app searched, matched and supplied in the evidence panel.
- Distinguish no matching text from excluded access or a failed search.
- Reject direct trade-date citations that conflict with the cited record.

## Access and limits

Retrieval is read-only and respects written-record privacy settings. Search covers records loaded in the app, not arbitrary files or every app database table. It uses text matching, not semantic search. A search without matches does not prove an event was never recorded in other words.

The coach cannot request additional records on its own or analyse screenshots in this release. Citation checks catch specific errors but do not guarantee that the model's interpretation is correct.

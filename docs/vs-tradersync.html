Frequently asked questions

Can I move my TraderSync trade history into TradeHelp?

Yes. Export your trades from TraderSync as a CSV file, then use TradeHelp's Local Inbox. The column mapper handles any CSV with headers, and duplicate rows are detected and skipped automatically.

Is TradeHelp really offline? What about the AI?

The journal itself never requires internet. The AI coach runs locally on your machine through Ollama, a free local model runner, so coaching works with the network cable unplugged and your trade data stays private. TraderSync's Cypher AI runs on their cloud and is metered per day (5 messages on Pro, 15 on Premium, 60 on Elite). Learn more on the offline journal page.

How much do I save with TradeHelp?

TraderSync costs $29.95 to $79.95 per month ($360-$960/year). TradeHelp costs $25-$50 once, saving you over $300 every year.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every download includes a 14-day free trial with all features unlocked: no account, no credit card. TraderSync has no free tier at all, only a 7-day trial (no credit card required). If TradeHelp fits your routine, one key activates up to 2 devices forever.

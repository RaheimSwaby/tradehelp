Frequently asked questions

Can I move my TraderSync trade history into TradeHelp?

Yes — export your trades from TraderSync as a CSV file, then use TradeHelp's Local Inbox. The column mapper handles any CSV with headers, and duplicate rows are detected and skipped automatically.

Is TradeHelp really offline? What about the AI?

The journal itself never requires internet. The AI coach runs locally on your machine through Ollama — a free, local model runner — so coaching works with the network cable unplugged. Learn more on the offline journal page.
